**Description** (4000):
```
Relief builds a strength plan around you and shows your progress on a 3D figure.

Answer a few questions about your goal, experience, equipment, schedule and injuries. It takes about a minute. Relief turns your answers into a 24-week plan in four phases, then adjusts it as you go.

YOUR PLAN
• Built for your goal: build muscle, lose fat, get stronger, get fitter or improve endurance
• Fits your week: 2–6 workouts, 20–90 minutes, gym or home, any equipment
• Clear progression: sets, reps and rest change phase by phase, with the reasons explained
• Gentler swaps and extra posture and feet exercises when your answers call for them

IN THE GYM
• Log sets in seconds, with last time's numbers next to each set
• A rest timer that tells you what's next
• Exercise directions and animations for every move
• One-sided moves are marked per leg or per arm
• Upright and recumbent bike intervals with seconds and rpm
• Skip an exercise and say why: Relief adapts the plan

YOUR BODY, IN 3D
• A chrome figure lights up the muscles you trained
• See how recovered each muscle is before your next workout
• Then and now: compare your body 1, 3 or 6 months ago with today

POSTURE CHECK
• Two photos and a few taps show what to work on: head and shoulder position, knees and arches
• The plan adds the right exercises for it
• A rough posture check from photos, not a medical diagnosis

PROGRESS
• Personal records, milestones and weekly streaks
• Weekly check-ins for weight, measurements and how the week went
• Progress photos that stay on your phone

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• No account, no ads, no tracking
• Everything stays on your phone and works offline
• Save a backup file whenever you want

Available in English, Ukrainian, Spanish, German, French, Portuguese and Polish.

Relief gives general fitness information, not medical advice. If you have a health condition, pain, or are pregnant, check with a doctor before starting a new exercise program.
```

## App Review Information (англійською, бачить лише рецензент)

- **Sign-in required:** No (зняти галочку).
- **Contact:** Oleksii Spektoruk · support.relief@gmail.com · твій телефон.
- **Notes:**
```
Relief has no account or sign-in and makes no network requests: all data is stored on the device.

To try it: tap "Set up my profile" and answer the questions (about a minute). The plan appears on the Plan tab and today's workout on the Today tab; tap Start to log sets. The Body tab shows trained muscles on the 3D figure.

The camera is used only for progress photos and the optional posture check (Me tab or Body tab). Photos stay on the device. The posture check is clearly labelled as a rough estimate from photos, not a medical diagnosis, and the plan's health guidance cites public guidelines (Plan tab → "What this plan is based on").
```
